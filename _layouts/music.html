Music

I've been playing music about as long as I've been breathing. Three of those projects are on Spotify — a duo with my twin, and two bands from college and beyond. Press play.

01

The Kelson Twins

americana duo with my twin sister · 2012 — 2020

An americana duo with my twin sister, Madeleine. Featuring tight harmonies, poetic lyrics, and the awkward onstage banter only siblings can have. We started writing songs at 12 and playing Chicago clubs at 15, raised over $10K on Indiegogo for our debut EP, Best of (So Far), and cut a Daytrotter session along the way.
