A pipeline that flags rising Google searches, checks whether your newsroom covered the topic, and measures the timing gap if it did. Trend detection in Python; article matching via a two-stage Jaccard pre-filter into Snowflake Cortex.

A self-directed engineering project, fully shown. The writeup covers the detection logic, the matcher, a false-positive case study, and how to wire the pipeline into your own newsroom's GSC and CMS.
