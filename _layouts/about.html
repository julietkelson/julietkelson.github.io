Hey
there

I'm a senior data analyst at the Minnesota Star Tribune, where I've spent the last six years working across the full analytics stack — tracking, experimentation, BI, and infrastructure — for teams in product, marketing, acquisition, and retention.

I like working on messy, cross-functional problems and the work underneath them — the untangling, the negotiating, the moment things finally click. Lately, figuring out how to use AI effectively and responsibly feels like exactly that kind of problem.

Outside of work, you can find me in my garden, trying to play hockey, sunbathing with my dog, or reading books on a Kindle my family said I'd never open — just to spite them.
